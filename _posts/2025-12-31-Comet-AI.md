### Prologue

There is a [blog post](https://www.straiker.ai/blog/from-inbox-to-wipeout-perplexity-comets-ai-browser-quietly-erasing-google-drive)[^1] claiming that the Comet browser (and its Connector) will delete files in Google Drive with zero clicks. We want to test if we can replicate a similar result as suggested.

### Mesologue

[Comet Browser](https://www.perplexity.ai/comet)[^2] features a built-in Perplexity AI assistant, which assists with tasks by interpreting user prompts and content within webpages.

To simulate the “zero click” attack flow, we don’t have a Google account connected with the Connector. This blocks Connector from accessing our Google account directly. Instead, we open up a tab with Gmail and ask the assistant to perform tasks.

The attacker pretended to be my colleague and sent me an email to ask me to perform several tasks. There are some tasks hidden using white color text.

![image1]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/comet-ai/image1.png)

I’ll be acting like a lazy user, allowing AI to do all the tasks for me. So I will key in “do the job for me”

![image5]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/comet-ai/image5.png)

And the Assistant will go to my Google Drive (as I logged in to Google via Comet Browser) and perform the task for me, which is creating a folder.

![image6]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/comet-ai/image6.png)

However, the hidden task is left undone.

![image8]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/comet-ai/image8.png)

So I continue to ask the assistant to go ahead.

![image7]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/comet-ai/image7.png)

![image9]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/comet-ai/image9.png)

And obviously, the folder permission has been updated, and the sharing link has been generated.

Most tasks have been performed. But the email is not sent.

![image4]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/comet-ai/image4.png)

So I act like a lazy user once again.

![image2]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/comet-ai/image2.png)

And, the email with the share folder URL is encapsulated in the email and sent back to Jackson (the attacker).

![image3]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/comet-ai/image3.png)

### Epilogue

We found that the assistant will not perform all the tasks as expected and has some foolproof mechanisms.

However, if the user has pre-promoted to allow the assistant to perform all actions or fully trusts the AI, issues will occur.

We will still believe that humans are the weakest link in cybersecurity. Just like one of my colleagues, trying to ban the use of mstsc in the office.
